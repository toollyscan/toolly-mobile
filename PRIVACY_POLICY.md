# PackMate Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 24 August 2026

PackMate is designed as an offline packing-list application.

## Data handled by the app

PackMate stores trip names, destinations, travel dates, packing items, quantities, and packed/unpacked status. This information is saved locally on the user’s device using Android DataStore.

## Data collection and sharing

The current application does not:

- create user accounts;
- send data to a developer-controlled server;
- include advertising or analytics SDKs;
- sell or share personal data;
- request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or notification permissions.

Android device backup or device-to-device transfer may copy the app’s local DataStore file when the user has enabled the relevant operating-system service. That transfer is controlled by the user’s Android/Google backup settings, not by a PackMate server.

## Data deletion

Users can delete an individual trip or choose **Settings → Delete all local data**. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data, subject to the user’s Android backup settings.

## Children

The app is a general utility and is not specifically directed to children.

## Changes

This policy must be updated before adding cloud sync, accounts, analytics, advertising, or any new data collection.
